# 03 — Vision

## Vision statement

For **neighborhood store owners (*tenderos*) in Neiva**, who need to **stop losing merchandise
to expirations and speed up daily checkout**, **Gestión Neiva (GIN)** is a **single-deployable
multi-tenant POS SaaS with in-app AI voice capabilities** that **tracks real-time stock, scans
barcodes via mobile camera, and prevents overselling**, unlike **paper notebooks and legacy ERPs**,
which **are expensive, cumbersome, and alienating to non-technical store clerks**.

## Objectives

| # | Objective | Metric |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Accelerate POS checkout speed | Under 15 seconds from barcode scan to completed transaction |
| 2 | Eliminate spoilage from untracked expirations | 100% of products within 15 days of expiration alerted on dashboard |
| 3 | Enforce strict multi-tenant data isolation | Zero cross-tenant data leaks (404/403 enforced on all queries) |
| 4 | Lower technology barrier with Voice AI | Under 3 seconds roundtrip for voice restocking and natural language queries |
| 5 | Maintain lean operational footprint | Single deployable serving first 100 stores with zero dedicated ops staff |

## Reviewing the vision

The vision is revisited quarterly. A new objective added between reviews requires stakeholder
agreement and must directly align with [`../01-context/scope.md`](../01-context/scope.md).
